# AMB Booking Desk

Manage booking enquiries using your business information and booking system.

Follow the [setup guide](https://bird.com/docs/ai/route-messages-to-an-agent#grok-bot), customise [bot.md](bot.md) and enable [amb-receive-reply](../../skills/amb-receive-reply/SKILL.md). Give the Bot [shared safety](../../shared/message-safety.md) as lasting instructions alongside `bot.md`.

Bird tools: `amb_get`, `amb_conversations_get`, `amb_conversations_list_messages`, `amb_send` (AMB read/write).

Change the settings by [editing your Bot in chat](https://cursor.com/docs/grok-bot/work#edit-a-bot).
